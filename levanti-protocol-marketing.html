Positioning

Frank Levanti's MAC-FeMoS₂@NiSe₂-BDD-ZIF-8-Cu-Ru system is pitched as one platform doing three jobs a buyer usually sources separately: hydrogen evolution, oxygen evolution, and CO₂ capture/valorization. The strategic story is consolidation, not novelty — fewer vendors, one stability benchmark (1,000+ hours), one integrated data layer.

One reactor, three revenue linesBankable stability dataSoftware-defined operations

Ideal Customer Profiles

  • Industrial offtake buyers — ports, shipping fuel depots, ammonia/methanol blenders, aquaculture operators needing O₂, community/microgrid generators.
  • Carbon buyers — compliance-market entities under emerging CBAM-style regimes, corporate voluntary-market ESG buyers wanting tech-removal (not just avoidance) credits.
  • Capital & policy — climate-tech investors, DOE/EU hydrogen-shot-adjacent grant programs, university/industry co-development partners.
  • Digital-services buyers — facility engineers and sustainability officers at any electrolyzer/DAC operator, not only Levanti hardware owners — the MRV/telemetry layer can sell standalone.

Channel Playbook

  • Thought leadership: publish the thesis findings as a technical whitepaper + preprint; submit to H2 industry trade press; this is the lowest-cost, highest-credibility channel and should run continuously, not as a single push.
  • Trade shows & conferences: live reactor demo booths at hydrogen/climate industrial expos generate the highest-quality enterprise offtake leads — budget for physical demo logistics.
  • ESG/impact PR: lead with third-party-verifiable SDG 7/9/13 metrics; this is what moves carbon-buyer trust and compliance-market eligibility, more than general awareness.
  • Paid digital + creator partnerships: best for the digital-services (SaaS) lane — cheaper CAC, faster cycle, works well for a telemetry/API product sold independent of hardware.
  • Policy & grant engagement: slow-burn, high-variance, but a single grant can fund a full deployment-site buildout; treat as portfolio insurance, not primary revenue.
  • Direct enterprise sales: outbound to named ports/utilities/aquaculture operators converts the slowest but produces the largest contracts — staff this as a dedicated motion once 2+ deployment sites are online.

Digital Services Packaging & Pricing Framework

  • Starter — single-reactor telemetry dashboard, uptime/degradation alerts. Priced for a single facility engineer to expense without approval friction.
  • Pro — adds the carbon MRV API (auditable capture/valorization reporting) and predictive-maintenance scoring against the 1,000-hour benchmark. Priced for a facility/operations budget line.
  • Enterprise — white-label multi-site fleet view, dedicated integration support, and contract-grade MRV exports for compliance filings. Priced per negotiated master agreement, not self-serve.
  • Land-and-expand: enter on Starter via a free 1,000-hour benchmark data teaser, upsell into Pro once a customer needs auditable numbers for a compliance or ESG filing.

90-Day Launch Sequence

  • Days 1–30: publish the whitepaper, open the Starter tier waitlist, begin continuous thought-leadership cadence.
  • Days 31–60: first trade-show presence, launch ESG/impact PR around initial SDG metrics, open Pro tier.
  • Days 61–90: begin direct enterprise outreach to the highest-fit offtake candidate in each product line (H₂, O₂, methanol, CO₂ credits), submit first grant/policy application, open Enterprise tier by invitation.
