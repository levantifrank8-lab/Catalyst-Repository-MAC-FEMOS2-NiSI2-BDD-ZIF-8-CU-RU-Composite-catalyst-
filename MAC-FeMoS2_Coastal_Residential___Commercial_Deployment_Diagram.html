⚗️ FeMoS₂ — HER Catalyst

Iron-doped molybdenum disulfide with carbon encapsulation drives the Hydrogen Evolution Reaction at low overpotential, even in corrosive seawater.

🟢 NiSe₂ — OER Catalyst

Nickel selenide enables the Oxygen Evolution Reaction, generating O₂ while preventing chlorine gas formation — critical for seawater electrolysis.

💎 BDD — Electron Highway

Boron-Doped Diamond provides a stable, corrosion-resistant conductive backbone for long-term operation (>1000 hrs) in harsh marine environments.

🔷 ZIF-8 — CO₂ Capture

Metal-Organic Framework selectively adsorbs CO₂ from building emissions and atmospheric streams, concentrating it at catalytic sites for conversion.

🔴 Cu-Ru — CO₂ Conversion

Copper initiates CO₂ reduction while Ruthenium drives hydrogenation, producing methanol, formate, and hydrocarbons — turning waste into value.

🏠 Residential Use Case

Coastal home uses seawater + solar PV to power the electrolyzer. H₂ stored in tank feeds fuel cell for 24/7 electricity and heating. CO₂ from kitchen/HVAC captured and valorized.

🏢 Commercial Use Case

Building-scale deployment with rooftop solar array and H₂ pipeline feeds. Higher CO₂ capture capacity from HVAC systems. Methanol by-product provides additional revenue stream.

🌊 Seawater Advantage

Eliminates freshwater dependency entirely. Operates across seawater, freshwater, and atmospheric moisture — transforming 71% of Earth's surface into H₂ production sites.
