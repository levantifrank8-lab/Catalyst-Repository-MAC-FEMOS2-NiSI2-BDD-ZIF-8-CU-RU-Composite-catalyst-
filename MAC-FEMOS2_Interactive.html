Thesis Statement The MAC-FeMoS₂@NiSe₂-BDD-ZIF-8-Cu-Ru composite catalyst represents a transformative breakthrough in sustainable energy technology by simultaneously addressing three critical global challenges: clean hydrogen production through efficient water splitting, CO₂ capture and utilization for climate mitigation, and resource flexibility through operation across diverse aquatic environments—demonstrating that rational multi-component materials design can transcend traditional catalytic limitations and provide practical pathways toward a carbon-neutral energy economy.

Broader Implications

Impact Across Three Domains

🔬 Scientific Impact
New Design Principles

Establishes interface engineering, electronic coupling, and spatial organization of incompatible reactions as a new paradigm—enabling cooperative catalysis impossible in single-phase materials.

⚡ Technological Impact
Green H₂ Infrastructure

Provides a practical platform for green hydrogen economy infrastructure, enabling decentralized production, renewable energy storage, and CO₂-neutral chemical manufacturing at scale.

🌍 Societal Impact
Global Equity & Survival

Addresses energy security, climate change, and water scarcity simultaneously. Contributes directly to equitable, sustainable development without forcing tradeoffs between economic growth and environmental protection.
