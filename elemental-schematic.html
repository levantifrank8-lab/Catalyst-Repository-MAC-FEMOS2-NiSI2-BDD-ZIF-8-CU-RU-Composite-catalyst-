How to read this: the electrolyzer stack (center) is the same MAC-FeMoS₂@NiSe₂-BDD cell from the thesis diagram, redrawn as a power-generation path. Renewable DC drives HER at the iron-doped MoS₂ cathode and OER at the NiSe₂ anode; boron-doped diamond forms the low-resistance current collectors on both faces. Rather than piping H₂ off-site, this configuration stores it briefly and feeds a co-located PEM fuel cell, converting it back to grid-ready electricity for a residential/commercial mix — useful for peaker-style dispatch, backup power, or sites without a hydrogen offtake buyer.
