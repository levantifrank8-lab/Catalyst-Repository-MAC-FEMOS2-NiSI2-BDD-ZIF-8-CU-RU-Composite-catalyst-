Six components, one building-scale unit
MAC-FeMoS2@C
Cathode · HER
Iron-doped MoS2 encapsulated in carbon. Drives hydrogen evolution at the electrolyzer cathode; carbon shell resists active-site poisoning over multi-year service life.
NiSe2
Anode · OER
Nickel diselenide anode catalyst. Drives oxygen evolution opposite the cathode, completing the water-splitting half-reactions.
Boron-Doped Diamond
Current Collector / Substrate
Conductive, corrosion-resistant electrode backbone. Carries current to both electrodes and tolerates seawater or brackish feed without degrading.
ZIF-8
CO2 Capture Framework
Zeolitic imidazolate framework (Zn + imidazolate). Selectively adsorbs CO2 from indoor air, flue gas, or HVAC exhaust for the parallel valorization loop.
Cu + Ru
CO2 Reduction & Hydrogenation
Copper initiates CO2 reduction and C–C coupling; ruthenium hydrogenates intermediates toward methanol and short hydrocarbons using a share of the on-site H2.
What the unit actually does on site

A building-scale H2-GRID unit is a sealed utility cabinet (residential) or rack row (commercial) containing the electrolyzer stack, hydrogen buffer storage, a fuel cell, and an optional CO2-valorization train. Renewable DC — rooftop solar, a small wind input, or grid off-peak power — drives the electrolyzer to split water into H2 and O2. The H2 is buffered in on-site storage and run through a fuel cell on demand, exporting AC power to the building panel; captured waste heat is routed to the domestic or facility hot-water loop. In parallel, a slipstream of H2 can feed the CO2 loop, converting captured CO2 into methanol for on-site use or collection, rather than treating carbon capture as a pure cost center.

Reading the diagram: the electrolyzer stack, storage, fuel cell, and heat-recovery loop are the power-generation core shared by both installation scales. The CO2 train underneath is optional and draws a slipstream of the same H2 rather than competing with the export path. Switching to Commercial Array above multiplies the stack count and storage rather than changing the underlying chemistry — it's the same six materials, replicated.

Why fuel-cell dispatch instead of grid injection of H2 gas: round-trip electrolyzer→storage→fuel-cell efficiency is inherently lower than direct solar-to-grid (typically ~35–50%). The building-scale case for this unit is dispatchability and resilience — shifting renewable production into evening peaks, riding through outages, and recovering waste heat — not competing with direct PV on a pure kWh-in/kWh-out basis.
