01
Six Components, One Stack
MAC-FeMoS₂
CATHODE — HER

Iron-doped MoS₂ encapsulated in amorphous carbon. Drives hydrogen evolution while the carbon shell resists fouling from dissolved organics and salts.

NiSe₂
ANODE — OER

Handles the oxygen half-reaction. Paired with the BDD backbone, it sustains anodic potentials in saline electrolyte without pitting.

BDD
SUBSTRATE — CONDUCTION

Boron-doped diamond forms the corrosion-proof electron highway linking both electrodes and rejecting chlorine evolution pathways.

ZIF-8
SORBENT — CO₂ CAPTURE

A porous metal-organic framework that selectively adsorbs CO₂ from ambient or flue-gas streams and concentrates it at the reaction core.

Cu
CO₂ REDUCTION — C–C COUPLING

Initiates CO₂ reduction and couples carbon centers toward multi-carbon products rather than stopping at CO.

Ru
HYDROGENATION FINISHER

Completes hydrogenation of Cu-activated intermediates into methanol, formate, and light hydrocarbons for storage or sale.

From Sealed Cell to Meter

Residential Delivery

One sealed unit intakes ambient water, splits it, and routes the recombined H₂/O₂ energy through a fuel cell stage to household load — no pipeline, no tanker, no freshwater draw.
