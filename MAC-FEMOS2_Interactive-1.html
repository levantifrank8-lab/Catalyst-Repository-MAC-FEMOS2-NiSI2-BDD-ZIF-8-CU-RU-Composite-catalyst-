Architecture

Six-Component Synergy

MAC
Component 01
Iron-Doped MoS₂ in Amorphous Carbon
Fe doping creates sulfur vacancies that serve as active hydrogen evolution sites. Carbon encapsulation prevents active site poisoning during extended operation.
HER Engine
NiSe₂
Component 02
Nickel Diselenide
Enables the anodic oxygen evolution reaction (OER), completing the full water-splitting cycle. Paired with MAC-FeMoS₂ for balanced HER/OER activity.
O₂ Generator
BDD
Component 03
Boron-Doped Diamond
Provides corrosion-resistant, ultrafast electron transport pathways — the "wiring" of the catalyst. Ensures stability in aggressive electrochemical environments including seawater.
Foundation & Wire
ZIF-8
Component 04
Zeolitic Imidazolate Framework
Porous MOF that selectively adsorbs CO₂ from dilute mixed-gas streams, concentrating it at catalytic reduction sites. Transforms the system from energy consumer to CO₂ processor.
CO₂ Manager
Cu
Component 05
Copper Catalyst
Initiates CO₂ reduction through C–C coupling reactions, producing multi-carbon hydrocarbons and alcohols. Cu's unique electronic structure enables selectivity control.
C–C Coupler
Ru
Component 06
Ruthenium Catalyst
Drives hydrogenation via Sabatier and Fischer-Tropsch processes, converting CO₂ + H₂ into methane, methanol, and long-chain hydrocarbons with high selectivity.
Hydrogenation Finisher
