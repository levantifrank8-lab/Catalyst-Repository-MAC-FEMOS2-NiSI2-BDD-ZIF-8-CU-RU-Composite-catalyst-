07
Formula → Physical Layout

The formula's own notation dictates the floor plan. The "@" between MAC-FeMoS₂ and NiSe₂ means the hydrogen and oxygen domains are built as a single interfaced heterostructure — in the case, that becomes two electrode blocks facing each other across a shared BDD backbone, which does double duty as the corrosion-proof structural spine and the electron highway connecting them.

The "-" chain that follows — BDD-ZIF-8-Cu-Ru — describes a sequence of support, capture, then conversion. That sequence is read top to bottom in the case: ZIF-8 nodules sit in the headspace where incoming water and dissolved air first pass through, and the Cu-Ru core sits at the base where whatever CO₂ that ZIF-8 has captured collects and gets converted.

Everything is sealed inside a single transparent, corrosion-resistant enclosure with one intake port — the same case shown filling with water in the reactor visualization.

08
Design → Working Prototype
01
Water intakeThe sealed case draws from whichever source is available on site — seawater, freshwater, or atmospheric moisture condensed by a coupled harvester — and fills to its operating level around the electrode stack.
02
Water splittingA renewable DC input drives the reaction: MAC-FeMoS₂ reduces water to hydrogen at the cathode (HER) while NiSe₂ oxidizes water to oxygen at the anode (OER), with BDD carrying current between them without corroding, even in saline electrolyte.
03
Parallel carbon captureWhile water splits, ZIF-8 in the headspace adsorbs CO₂ from the intake stream or entrained air and concentrates it toward the base of the case.
04
CO₂ conversionCaptured CO₂ reaches the Cu-Ru core, where copper couples carbon centers together and ruthenium hydrogenates the result into methanol, formate, or light hydrocarbons — a sellable by-product stream, not waste.
05
Electricity deliveryCollected H₂ and O₂ are recombined through a fuel-cell stage to deliver power on demand. A single sealed unit is sized for a residential kW-scale load; racked banks of units scale the same design up to a commercial facility's baseload.
